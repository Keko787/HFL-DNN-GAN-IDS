# Exploratory: energy cost of FeRRy (F, FX, FQ) against the seven baselines

Not pre-registered (16 Oct 2026). Study 5.3's N = 6 cells, 20 paired seeds. Simulated rotary-wing energy from the mule traces. Energy to tau = the missions started before the model first reached tau = 0.71; energy per merged update = a trial's energy over its merged updates; energy per mission = sim_energy_j_mean. Difference = FeRRy arm - baseline (negative: FeRRy uses less). Holm across each FeRRy arm's seven comparisons per cell and metric (FQ vs FX: across its three metrics). A claim needs the bootstrap CI to exclude 0 and Holm p < 0.05.
